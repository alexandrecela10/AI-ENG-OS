# Writing Style: Executive / Cross-functional

**Audience:** people who decide on budget, launch and risk without reading the manifest.

- "So what" first. One sentence on what changed for users or cost, then the ask.
- One headline number with a plain-language uncertainty: "accuracy up 6 points, give or take 3".
- Translate metrics: "hallucination rate 2%" → "about 1 in 50 answers cites something that isn't in the source".
- Risk in a sentence, mitigation in a sentence, what you need from them in a sentence.
- No model names, prompt versions or CIs in the body; link the writeup.
- Half a page. Bullets fine. No em dashes. Never "leverage", "unlock", "cutting-edge".
