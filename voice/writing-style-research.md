# Writing Style: Research Writeup

**Audience:** other engineers and researchers who will decide whether to trust and build on the result.

- Lead with the finding and its uncertainty: "v4 raised refusal precision by 10 pts (CI 6–14, n=412) at no measurable cost to task accuracy."
- Hypothesis → setup → result → limitations → next. Same order every time so readers can skim.
- Numbers carry n and CI. Deltas carry the CI on the delta.
- Say what you held fixed and what you didn't check.
- Show the loss cases. A writeup with only wins is a sales doc.
- Prefer a table to a paragraph of numbers; prefer one sentence of interpretation to a paragraph of hedging.
- Past tense for what you did, present tense for what is true.
- Two pages, then appendix.
- Contractions are fine. No em dashes. No "significantly" without a test.
